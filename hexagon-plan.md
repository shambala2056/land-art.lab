# HEXAGON — Construction and Planting Sequence

Land-art Space · Shambala Carbon Offsets LLC
Dornogovi aimag, Erdene sum

---

## 1 · Purpose

This document establishes the order in which the 54 cells of the HEXAGON installation are
surveyed, built and planted. The sequence is dependency-driven: each phase creates a physical
or biological precondition required by the phase that follows. Departing from the order does
not accelerate delivery — it transfers cost into the maintenance period as replanting.

## 2 · Governing sequencing logic

Three constraints determine the order of works. Every item in Section 4 derives from one of them.

**2.1 · Water and power precede everything.** No cell — structural or planted — can be
commissioned before the borehole is producing and the pump has a permanent power source. The
water and solar cells are therefore the project's critical path from day one.

**2.2 · Heavy works precede delicate works.** Construction traffic, excavation and material
handling are incompatible with establishing seedlings. All buildings, fencing and pipework are
completed before the first tree is planted, not around it.

**2.3 · Hardy species precede demanding species.** The pioneer plantings are not filler. Saxaul
stabilises sand and reduces ground-level wind velocity; Caragana is a nitrogen-fixing legume
that raises soil fertility for the species planted after it. Siberian elm placed on open sand
without this preparation fails in its third summer. Habitat structures follow the same rule:
forage before shelter.

## 4 · Work sequence

### Phase 0 — Survey and set-out

Site conditions determine cell placement, not the design drawing. Salinity and depth to
groundwater decide where Tamarix is viable and where elm is not.

| # | Work item | Predecessor | Gate / deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1 | Hydrogeological survey — depth, yield, salinity | — | Borehole location fixed |
| 0.2 | Soil analysis across 5–6 zones | 0.1 | Species-to-zone allocation |
| 0.3 | Topographic survey | — | Low-lying and elevated ground identified |
| 0.4 | GPS set-out of all 54 cells | 0.2, 0.3 | Cell register: ID, type, size, coordinates |

**Phase gate:** issued-for-construction site plan with every cell located, typed and sized.

### Phase 1 — Water and power

| # | Work item | Cell | Predecessor | Gate / deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Borehole drilling | Water storage & dew field | 0.1 | Yield and water quality test report |
| 1.2 | Solar array installation | Solar field | 0.4 | Array energised |
| 1.3 | Pump installation and commissioning | Water storage & dew field | 1.1, 1.2 | Sustained pumping demonstrated |
| 1.4 | Storage tanks and dew-capture field | Water storage & dew field | 1.3 | Buffer volume in service |
| 1.5 | Trunk main and cell distribution network | All | 0.4, 1.4 | Pressure test passed at every cell head |

**Note on 1.1–1.3:** drilling is carried out on temporary diesel plant, but the production pump
requires permanent power. Until the solar array is energised the borehole is a hole, not a water
source. Items 1.1 and 1.2 therefore run in parallel.

**Phase gate:** water available at pressure at every cell head.

### Phase 2 — Enclosure and soil

| # | Work item | Cell | Predecessor | Gate / deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Perimeter fencing, full 3 ha | Site-wide | 0.4 | Livestock exclusion certified |
| 2.2 | Greenhouse and composting facility | Compost & greenhouse | 1.5 | Facility operational |
| 2.3 | Seedling propagation begins | Compost & greenhouse | 2.2 | First batch under cultivation |
| 2.4 | Soil cultivation and compost incorporation | Planted cells | 2.2 | Cells prepared, by zone |

**Note on 2.2:** the greenhouse is production infrastructure, not amenity. It supplies seedlings
and soil amendment to every subsequent planting cell. Deferring it forces all planting stock to
be bought in and transported, which raises unit cost and reduces establishment rates.

**Phase gate:** site enclosed; propagation running; planting beds prepared.

### Phase 3 — Buildings

| # | Work item | Cell | Predecessor | Gate / deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Hub building and laboratory | Hub building & laboratory | 1.5, 2.1 | Structure weathertight and serviced |
| 3.2 | Community dormitory | Community dormitory | 1.5, 2.1 | Occupancy ready |
| 3.3 | Laboratory fit-out and instrumentation | Hub building & laboratory | 3.1 | Baseline measurement capability in place |

**Note:** buildings are completed before any planting. Construction traffic through established
cells causes losses that are attributed to the planting programme but caused by the build
programme.

**Phase gate:** site operational and staffed; baseline measurement capability commissioned.

### Phase 4 — Pioneer planting

Planted strictly in hardiness order, working from the windward edge inward.

| # | Work item | Cell | Predecessor | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | Saxaul planting | Saxaul | 2.4, 3.2 | Windward perimeter, elevated ground |
| 4.2 | Caragana planting | Caragana | 4.1 | Second rank, behind Saxaul |
| 4.3 | Tamarix planting | Tamarix | 2.4 | Saline and low-lying ground |
| 4.4 | Irrigation connection and first watering cycle | 4.1–4.3 | 1.5 | Each cell logged as commissioned |

**Phase gate:** pioneer cells established and irrigated; wind velocity and sand movement recorded
against the pre-planting baseline.

### Phase 5 — Secondary planting

Executed once the pioneer plantings have established, typically one to two growing seasons after
Phase 4.

| # | Work item | Cell | Predecessor | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | Flowering species planting | Pollinators hive | 4.2 | Adjacent to shrub cells |
| 5.2 | Siberian elm planting | Elm | 4.2 | Sheltered interior |
| 5.3 | Mongolian chives planting | Mongolian chives | 5.2 | Understorey, partial shade |

**Note on 5.2:** elm is the signature canopy species and the most demanding. Planted behind the
Saxaul and Caragana ranks, it draws on reduced wind exposure and improved soil nitrogen — the
conditions on which the 80% survival target depends.

**Phase gate:** full planting schedule complete; first survival census recorded.

### Phase 6 — Habitat structures

| # | Work item | Cell | Predecessor | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6.1 | Hive installation | Pollinators hive | 5.1, first flowering | Forage must precede colonisation |
| 6.2 | Bird hotel construction and installation | Bird hotel | 5.2, canopy establishment | Nesting structures require adjacent cover |

**Note:** habitat structures do not attract fauna on their own. Pollinators require forage in
flower; birds require insect populations and adjacent cover. Both follow the plantings that
support them, not the reverse.

**Phase gate:** habitat structures installed and under observation.

### Phase 7 — Monitoring and reporting

Continuous from Phase 3 commissioning onward.

| # | Work item | Frequency | Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7.1 | Irrigation control and telemetry | Continuous | Automated, solar-powered |
| 7.2 | Seedling survival census | Twice yearly | Full count, every planted cell |
| 7.3 | Land productivity (NDVI) | Annual | Satellite trend against pre-planting baseline |
| 7.4 | Land cover change | Annual | Satellite classification, ground-truthed |
| 7.5 | Soil organic carbon | Every 3 years | Core sampling, accredited laboratory |
| 7.6 | Biodiversity indicators | Annual | Pollinators, decomposers, small mammals, nesting birds |
