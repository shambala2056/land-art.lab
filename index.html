Ecosystem
A Web,
Not a Chain

An ecosystem is a network with feedback, not a one-way chain — plants depend on pollinators, and pollinators on plants. The programme is built the same way. Three units act at different points in that network, a fourth verifies what actually changed, and every claim below is tied to a published method.

Investment Structure

Core idea: use land art to restore desertified regions and reduce the number of ecological refugees displaced by drought and dzud. The restored land then supports education for local children and the conditions for them to work at home afterwards. Funding is not an abstract donation — every USD 30 is tied to one square metre of restored land, an NFC card, and a published cost breakdown. The full 10,000 m² — one hectare — is divided into hexagonal cells sized by contribution, from 1 m² up to 100 m². Every cell has a fixed location on site and carries its steward's name. Sponsorship confers stewardship and recognition, not title to land.

One Hectare.
Ten Thousand Cells.

The artwork is one hectare — 10,000 cells of one square metre. Sponsoring a cell is not a land purchase and not an open-ended donation: it funds a defined stack of costs, published in full below. This is why USD 30 per m² is not a restoration cost-per-hectare benchmark and should not be read as one.
