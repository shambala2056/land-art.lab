What this is

A membership you can walk onto

An organisation with a tree standing on the HEXAGON is already a member. Nothing else is asked of it. The contribution is located — a numbered cell on three fenced hectares at Erdene sum, reported on by itself — so membership rests on something that can be visited, measured and argued with, rather than on a pledge.

Between the meetings

Most of what is agreed at a conference is lost in the months after it, not during it. Parties who never got past a handshake have nowhere to go once the halls empty. This stays open the rest of the year.

Offers and needs, stated plainly

Every member writes two things: what it can offer, and what it is looking for. Field sites, co-funding, monitoring capacity, seed, expertise. Read one against the other and the match is obvious or it is not — no brokering required.

Measured, not asserted

The figures above were produced by an outside assessor against an unrestored control site, not by us. Members are invited to hold each other to the same standard, and to publish what did not work.

Bigger than any one cell

One organisation restores a hectare. Forty organisations comparing methods, co-funding trials and verifying each other's results move faster than forty working alone. That is the whole of the idea.
